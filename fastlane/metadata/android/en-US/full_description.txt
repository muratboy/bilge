An Android app that lets you learn any topic level by level, through play. You choose the topic, an AI generates the content based on a template, and Bilge turns it into a game.

Features:
- Courses on any topic, tailored to your level.
- Have any AI chat write a course from a template, or generate it directly within the app using your own API key. (Direct generation via API is optional and requires an internet connection.)
- 12 exercise types such as multiple choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, matching, ordering, and flashcards; support for formulas, charts, and text-to-speech.
- Levels, XP, streaks, badges, and the ability to test out of units you already know.
- Spaced repetition: due questions and past mistakes resurface in daily reviews.
- Lessons work completely offline; no accounts, ads, or tracking.
- English and Turkish interface, light and dark themes.
- Share courses as a single file or a bundle.

How to create a course:
1. Enter your topic and level.
2. Copy the template and paste it into any AI chat (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini), or generate directly using your API key.
3. Paste the output into Bilge.

Bilge features a team of seven mascots to accompany you on your learning journey. Your progress is stored only on your device.
